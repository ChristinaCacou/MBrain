# Novelty and prior-art assessment (targeted, not exhaustive)
Audit date: 21 October 2026

**Scope.** The paper presents a computational counterexample where two physically separate stimulus-response modules share a nuisance physiological state. Their output passes three already-specified statistical tests of cross-modal state transfer although no directed pathway connects the modules. This is a counterexample for the *specified tests and comparator*, not a refutation of integrated information theory, clinical PCI, or consciousness in organisms without nervous systems.

| Prior work | What it already establishes | Specific delta here | Remaining novelty risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pearl, *Causality*, 2nd ed. (2009) | Observational equivalence and need for valid interventions; confounding by common cause | Worked application to a fixed multimodal aneural assay and code-level diagnostic | The general non-identification result is standard, not a new theorem |
| Casali et al. *Sci Transl Med* 5:198ra105 (2013), doi:10.1126/scitranslmed.3006294 | Cortical TMS-EEG perturbational complexity (PCI) experimentally benchmarks levels of consciousness | Tests generic dimensionality / LZ features in a synthetic single-cell response system | Current features are **not** PCI; cannot claim to disprove it |
| Sarasso et al. *Neuroscience of Consciousness* (2021), doi:10.1093/nc/niab023 | Perturbational methods aim to avoid spurious common drivers and correlated inputs | Explicit failure example for a particular three-gate cross-modal prediction pipeline | Recognition of common-drive artifacts is prior art |
| Seth & Bayne, *Nat Rev Neurosci* 23, 439–452 (2022), doi:10.1038/s41583-022-00587-4 | Different consciousness theories require differentiating predictions | Specific executable stress test and proposed selectivity check | Non-specificity as a broad message is not new |
| Doan et al., bioRxiv updated 10 September 2026, doi:10.64898/2026.02.25.708045 | Stentor associative learning with relevant controls, and their own original interpretation | Conservative secondary reanalysis of one uploaded common-test experiment focusing on early/late response change and batch overlap | Secondary analysis is not independent replication of a causal mechanism |
| Rajan et al. 2026 + Dryad, doi:10.5061/dryad.z34tmpgrm | Published Stentor learning-related molecular changes | Explicit missing time-matched control sensitivity | Required control data not yet quantified |

**Verdict.** The unique contribution must be framed as an *adversarial, executable assay-specific falsification* of a particular mechanistic comparator, plus transparent biological illustrations. The novelty of a general common-cause confound or interventional non-identifiability is **not** established. A scholarly expert and broader systematic prior-art search are still needed before asserting priority, uniqueness, or Nature-level generality.
