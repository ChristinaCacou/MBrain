# Targeted novelty and prior-art assessment (19 October 2026)

Scope: search-guided comparison against selected directly relevant publications, not an exhaustive systematic review, patent clearance, or proof of priority.

## Prior art and overlap
1. Seth & Bayne (2022), *Theories of consciousness*, Nature Reviews Neuroscience, doi:10.1038/s41583-022-00587-4. Established difficulty of distinguishing consciousness theories and the need for discriminating predictions. Therefore the general demand for falsifiable mechanisms is NOT new.
2. COGITATE Consortium (2025), *Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness*, Nature, doi:10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1. Preregistered competing predictions have already been tested empirically. The 'adversarial comparison' methodology is NOT new.
3. Butlin et al. (2023), *Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence: Insights from the Science of Consciousness*, arXiv:2308.08708. Theory-derived computational indicators predate this manuscript. Claiming invention of indicator-based evaluation would be inaccurate.
4. Casali et al. (2013), *A theoretically based index of consciousness independent of sensory processing and behavior*, Science Translational Medicine, doi:10.1126/scitranslmed.3006294. PCI was developed for human brain perturbation, and this paper does NOT reproduce clinical PCI or falsify its validated operating characteristics.
5. Doan et al. (2026), *Associative learning in the protozoan Stentor coeruleus*, bioRxiv doi:10.64898/2026.02.25.708045. This study collected the living-cell data; the present paper contributes secondary inference and does not claim first demonstration of Stentor habituation or associative learning.
6. Ramdas et al. (2026), *A quantitative portrait of habituation in Stentor coeruleus*, bioRxiv doi:10.64898/2026.06.09.731162. Models Stentor habituation with hierarchical memory. A basic habituation model is not original here.
7. Pearl (2009), *Causality*, and Hernán & Robins (2020), *Causal Inference: What If*. Observational non-identifiability and requirements for valid interventions are established results; the Gaussian construction applies them, rather than discovering new causal mathematics.

## Defensible contribution
A constructed common-physiology null is applied to an exact, frozen *Stentor*-specific three-gate test and makes every gate pass despite no directed pathway between modality modules. A matched no-change control fails. This is a reproducible counterexample about mechanistic specificity of that TEST BATTERY, not a universal theorem disproving consciousness-associated markers or consciousness in aneural organisms. The synthetic effect is designed rather than independently fit or blindly prospectively registered.

## What remains unproven
- The proposed null has not been calibrated from *Stentor* ionic/metabolic recordings.
- Four simulated day clusters are insufficient to validate day-level Type I error from the frozen cell-level tests.
- The direction-of-effect in independent Doan habituation data does not identify an integration pathway.
- Recovery-time RNA controls have not been quantified and Miao gene-level replication was not completed.
- This targeted search cannot establish global novelty; journal referees may know closer counterexamples.

## Most defensible article positioning
Methods/analysis paper about mechanistic specificity and causal non-identification; clinical PCI is not tested or invalidated. A Nature-level original research claim still requires stronger independent biological or externally calibrated validation.
